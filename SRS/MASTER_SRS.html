Executive Summary

Enterprise Online Examination & Assessment Platform (EOEAP) is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for educational institutions with Super Admin, College Admin, Faculty and Student portals.

Project Goals

Provide secure assessment lifecycle, analytics, question bank, evaluation, reporting, QR/Guest/Recovery assessments, enterprise dashboards and scalable architecture.

College Management

  • Multi-step registration wizard.
  • Collect: College Name, Code, Address, Contacts, Website.
  • Dynamic Streams with Add/Remove Stream.
  • Unlimited batches under each stream with joining month/year.
  • Review page must show entered data.
  • Use clean URLs and session/draft state.
  • College Dashboard with dynamic analytics.

Dynamic College Dashboard

  • Independent dashboard per college (/admin/colleges/{id}).
  • KPIs: Students, Faculty, Streams, Programs, Assessments, Results.
  • Performance by Stream, Department, Program and Batch.
  • Assessment timeline with Date, Day, Time, Submitted, Pending, Guest, QR and Recovery.
  • Health Score and AI insights placeholders.

Assessment Studio

  • Create→Draft→Review→Ready→Publish workflow.
  • Scheduling, QR Access, Guest Access, Recovery Assessment.
  • Auto-save, Auto-submit, Grace period, Versioning.

Business Rules

  • College → Streams → Batches → Students.
  • Each dashboard must be filtered by College ID.
  • No cross-college data.
  • Soft delete only.
  • Audit every administrative action.
  • No sensitive data in URLs.
