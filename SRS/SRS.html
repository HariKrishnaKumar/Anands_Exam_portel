Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

Project Vision

Enterprise Online Examination & Assessment Platform with multi-tenant architecture, college onboarding, dynamic analytics, enterprise dashboards and future AI capabilities.

Major Functional Requirements

  • Authentication & RBAC
  • Super Admin Dashboard
  • College Registration Wizard
  • Dynamic College Dashboard
  • Student, Faculty & Assessment Management
  • Question Bank
  • Results & Evaluation
  • Guest/QR/Recovery Assessment
  • Analytics & Reports
  • Audit Logs

College Registration Wizard

  • Collect college profile (name, address, contacts, institution type).
  • Dynamic Streams with Add/Remove Stream.
  • Unlimited Batches under each Stream with joining month/year.
  • Review page shows all entered data dynamically.
  • Clean URLs using server-side wizard state.

Dynamic College Dashboard

  • Route: /admin/colleges/{college_id}
  • Every KPI, chart and report filtered by selected College ID.
  • Performance by Stream, Department, Program and Batch.
  • Assessment timeline with date, day, time, submitted, pending, QR, Guest and Recovery metrics.

UI/UX

  • Enterprise SaaS design
  • Apple-inspired filled icons
  • No duplicate icons
  • Responsive layouts
  • Dark/Light themes
