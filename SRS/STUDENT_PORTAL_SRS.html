Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

Student Portal

This document defines the complete Student Portal and how it integrates with the Super Admin, College Admin, Faculty, Assessment and Results modules.

1. Purpose

The Student Portal is the primary workspace for students to view academic information, attend assessments, monitor progress, download certificates, and communicate with faculty. Every student belongs to exactly one College, Stream, Batch, Program and Semester created from the Admin Portal.

2. Dependency on Admin Portal

  • Students cannot exist until a College is created.
  • Students inherit the academic hierarchy: College → Stream → Batch → Program → Semester.
  • Assessment availability depends on Admin/Faculty publishing.
  • Results are visible only after Admin/Faculty publishes them.
  • Student permissions are controlled by Admin RBAC.

5. Assessment Workflow

  1. Admin/Faculty publishes assessment.
  2. Student receives notification.
  3. Assessment appears on Dashboard and My Assessments.
  4. Student starts assessment after validation.
  5. Autosave runs throughout.
  6. Submission or auto-submit on timer expiry.
  7. Evaluation.
  8. Result publication.
  9. Performance analytics updated.

7. Business Rules

  • Students only view data belonging to their college.
  • Students cannot modify assessments.
  • Only published results are visible.
  • Recovery assessments require admin approval.
  • Guest access expires automatically.
  • Every action is logged.
